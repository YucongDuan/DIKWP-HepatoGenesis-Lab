01 / Same present observation, different futures

The low, middle and high histories start with the same capacity and reserve. Only structural burden differs. The chart shows computed capacity, not a medical outcome.

02 / Let competing explanations lose

Dynamic history, frozen history and erased history are fitted on complete training trajectories. Model selection is frozen on development data before the audit trajectories are evaluated. The known synthetic generator favors the dynamic model; the negative control tests whether complexity can be rejected.

03 / One policy, not one hidden-state oracle per case
Both hidden states are individually controllable. With indistinguishable observations, their required actions conflict. A common policy does not exist in the fixture. Revealing the state changes the information structure and restores finite-model feasibility.

04 / The ledger must close
The transport chain and well-mixed compartment use the same total volume, flow and reaction rate. Spatial resolution changes the output. Concentration is in mmol/L; transported amount is in mmol. The separate reaction audit uses J/mol, mol/s and kelvin.
Information quantity versus decoder meaning


05 / Choose a measurement that separates models
This finite design ranks candidate measurement times and channels by the worst pairwise standardized separation per declared cost unit. It is not a claim that the measurement establishes causality or is appropriate for patients.


06 / Corrections propagate
A synthetic source correction reopens every dependent model, result, claim and experimental purpose. A stored reviewer name is not a verified identity; a checksum is not proof of truth.
